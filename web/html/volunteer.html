Why Volunteer

Serving as a volunteer firefighter gives you the chance to make a tangible difference in your community. You will acquire essential lifelong skills in emergency response, teamwork, and leadership, all while becoming part of a close-knit department family.

What is Expected?

Volunteers are expected to attend regular weekly training sessions, respond to emergency calls when available, and assist with maintenance around the station. No prior experience is needed—we provide all training and gear.
