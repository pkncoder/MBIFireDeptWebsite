Established in...

For over a century, our station has stood as a beacon of safety and dedication. Founded by a small group of local citizens who recognized the need for coordinated fire response, our organization has grown from a single hand-drawn hose cart to a fully equipped modern department.

Today We...

Today, our department protects thousands of residents across our service area. Operating 24/7/365, our brave volunteer firefighters answer hundreds of calls each year ranging from structure fires and medical emergencies to motor vehicle accidents and hazardous condition responses.

How Do We...

We rely on a combination of local government funding, community grants, and generous personal donations. Our department is 100% volunteer-run, meaning every member donates their time to protect their neighbors without financial compensation.
